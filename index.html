Upload your Excel roadmap as CSV. Matched rows update status & notes. New rows are added to Library under their pillar's category.

How to use: Open your Excel → Save As → CSV UTF-8 → upload here.
Columns expected: #, Pillar, Category, Item, Provider, Description, Priority, Timeline, Cost, Status, Notes
Matching: Existing rows matched by title → status and notes updated. Blank Status cells leave existing completion untouched.
New rows: Any title that doesn't match is added as a custom resource under its pillar's category.
